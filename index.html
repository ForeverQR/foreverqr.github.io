Why it never expires

Most "free" QR sites
rent you your own link.

They hand you a dynamic code that points to their server, which then forwards to your link. When the trial ends, they cut the forwarding and charge you to turn it back on.

Dynamic QR (the trap)

Scans go through a middleman you don't control.

Scan→their-server.com/x7k→your link
  • ✕ Dies when the trial ends
  • ✕ Dies if the company shuts down
  • ✕ Printed posters become useless

Static QR (ForeverQR)

Your link is written into the pattern itself.

Scan→your link
  • ✓ Works for as long as your link works
  • ✓ Works even if this site disappears
  • ✓ No account, no tracking, no fees

Questions

Free QR codes, answered.

Is ForeverQR free?

Yes. Making and downloading QR codes is free, with no sign-up and no watermark. Pro downloads (photo QR, poster, SVG, transparent PNG, bulk) are unlocked free by following an Instagram page or watching an 8-second preview.

Do ForeverQR codes expire?

No. ForeverQR makes static QR codes: your link is written into the pattern itself, so the code keeps working for as long as your link does, even if this website disappears.

Can I make a UPI payment QR code?

Yes. Enter your UPI ID, name and an optional amount. Anyone can scan it with a UPI app and pay you directly.

Can I add my logo to the QR code?

Yes. Upload a PNG or JPG and it sits in the centre. Choose your colours, gradient, dot style and a "Scan me" frame.

Is my data stored?

No. Codes are made in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to a server.
